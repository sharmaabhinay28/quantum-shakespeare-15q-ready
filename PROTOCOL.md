## Question and limits

Test the experiment proposed in Abhinay Sharma's published article, https://medium.com/@sharmaabhinay28/stop-porting-gpu-training-to-quantum-computers-fd7cf5cf3bdb. Compare an IQP Born machine with its stochastic correlated-bitflip control, a small parameter-matched classical generator, and a smoothed frequency table. This is an unconditional three-character distribution experiment. No hardware, language-understanding, or computational-advantage claim is permitted.

## Models and fitting budget

Predeclare five fit seeds: 11, 23, 37, 53, 71. Run every model for every seed and encoding. Use IQPopt from https://github.com/XanaduAI/iqpopt pinned to commit `18ae04255ed80a274d8c49ed91cd4e6214757ccd`, package metadata version 2024.7.0. Archive the installed package version, commit, dependency lock, Python version, and platform. Use CPU execution and float64. Do not migrate to another package.

The IQP circuit has 15 wires, 15 single-wire X generators, and all 105 distinct two-wire XX generators. Each generator has its own angle, for 120 trainable parameters. Use IQPopt's `IqpSimulator` and `mmd_loss_iqp`, with its Gaussian kernel sigma values [1.0, 2.0, 4.0] averaged equally. Use 128 sampled operators, 128 estimator samples, and fresh random keys per optimizer step. Initialize angles independently from Uniform(-0.1, 0.1). Train with Adam, 500 updates, and batches of 512 training chunks sampled with replacement from the full training histogram. The three candidate learning rates are 0.01, 0.03, 0.1. Evaluate exact validation log loss after the final update of each candidate. Select the lowest validation log loss, with ties within 1e-12 resolved by the listed candidate order. Do not use test outcomes for selection or early stopping.

The stochastic model uses the identical generator list, initialization distribution, fit seeds, optimizer, steps, batches, bandwidths, and learning-rate candidates, with IQPopt `bitflip=True`. Its exact output distribution starts at the all-zero state and applies each generator's correlated XOR mask with probability sin(angle)^2. Validate this independent exact evaluator against the package's analytic bitflip expectations on small circuits.

The small classical model is an autoregressive Bernoulli model over the 15 bits. Each bit has a bias and one logistic weight for every preceding bit. This gives 15 biases plus 105 weights, or 120 parameters. Initialize weights and biases from Normal(0, 0.1). Train by minibatch negative log likelihood with Adam, the same three learning rates, 500 updates, and batches of 512 training chunks. Select by exact validation log loss using the same rule. The different training objective is explicit and must be discussed.

The frequency table uses all training chunk counts, add-alpha smoothing across the 32,768 states, and three alpha candidates: 0.01, 0.1, 1.0. Select by exact validation log loss with the same tie rule. Fit it separately for each seed and encoding for accounting, but state that its estimate is deterministic and repeated seeds are not independent evidence. The table has 32,767 independent probabilities and is intentionally not parameter matched.

Each model receives exactly three candidate configurations per seed and encoding. Thus each model has 45 candidate fits and 15 selected fits. Report optimizer steps and actual resources; do not claim equal compute merely because search counts match. No tuning based on test results, no extra restarts, and no stronger or weaker baseline introduced after test inspection.
